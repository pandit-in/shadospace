# Acceptable Use Policy

_Last Updated: 2026-07-10_

## Introduction

This document outlines the acceptable use policy for Shadospace. Users must not engage in activities that involve malware, spam, or disruptions of service.

## Prohibited Activities

- Distribution of malicious software.
- Sending unsolicited bulk messages (spam).
- Attempting to degrade or disrupt the service.

## Enforcement

Violations may result in account suspension or termination.
